# FIRM-4B v1 findings and v2 plan

## v1 status

FIRM-4B v1 is the first real specialist fine-tune: Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B revision
`851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a` with a rank-8 MLP LoRA trained
for 1,100 optimizer steps. The final adapter is retained under
`runs/gpu-results/firm4b-provisional-v1/final/` and the working quantized
Ollama artifact is installed locally as `firm:4b-v1`.

v1 demonstrated useful scope control and concise specialist style, but it is not
scientifically release-ready.

### Observed v1 strengths

- Reliably declined unrelated entertainment/personal requests in manual stress testing.
- Gave reasonable high-level explanations for MBE, MOCVD, passivation, Te, and multi-pole detector time constants.
- Learned the desired concise technical tone.
- Completed 1,100/1,100 steps with clean frozen-weight boundaries.
- In-distribution validation converged strongly, proving the adapter could learn its curriculum.

### Observed v1 failures

- Ambiguous response to MCT instead of defaulting to mercury cadmium telluride inside FIRM's domain.
- False refusal / malformed response to a photodetector taxonomy prompt, especially with a missing space typo.
- Weak or incorrect detector-material taxonomy.
- Incorrect InGaAs identity/composition.
- Incorrect or evasive Cd mole-fraction reasoning from HgCdTe cutoff.
- Incorrect HgCdTe substrate guidance.
- Incorrect NEP/D* explanation, including spurious wavelength/unit claims.
- NASD misread as NASDAQ rather than noise amplitude spectral density.
- Hallucinated model-designer attribution.
- Too much confidence in several wrong material statements.

## Diagnosis

The v1 dataset was structurally imbalanced for the desired product:
- 2,640 strict-JSON numerical examples trained equation execution but did not
  reliably transfer to natural conversational formula use.
- Reweighted scope/refusal rows were numerous enough to produce strong refusal
  behavior, including false refusals on malformed but valid domain prompts.
- Only 51 conceptual cards covered a very large infrared/semiconductor domain.
- Validation was mainly same-family/paraphrase validation and therefore
  overestimated domain generalization.
- The rank-8 MLP adapter was not obviously capacity-limited, but a larger rank
  with a lower LR is a reasonable v2 experiment after the data imbalance is fixed.

## v2 curriculum

Generated by `scripts/build_firm4b_v2_foundation.py`.

Current locked local manifest:
- 3,556 train examples
- 309 validation examples
- 84 canonical fact families
- 1,344 canonical-fact paraphrase/robustness rows total
- 2,355 natural-language, programmatically verified quantitative rows total
- 116 diagnostic-reasoning rows total
- 24 taxonomy rows total
- 26 unrelated scope-refusal rows total
- zero exact overlap with public FIRM stress/scope eval prompts
- private 11,500-question benchmark is never read by the builder

Coverage includes:
- HgCdTe/MCT, CdZnTe/CZT, InSb, InGaAs, InAs/InAsSb, T2SLs, QWIPs,
  lead salts and thermal detector materials.
- LPE, MBE, MOCVD, substrates, passivation, annealing, contacts and etching.
- Responsivity, QE, NEP, D*, NASD/PSD/ASD, Johnson/shot/G-R/1/f noise,
  Hall transport, cutoff/bandgap and time constants.
- QCL/ICL mid-IR sources, chalcogenide waveguides/fibers, MIR sensing,
  microbolometers, ROIC/FPA, NETD/BLIP, cold shields and radiometry.
- SRH/Auger/radiative recombination, tunneling, diffusion, depletion,
  étendue, Fresnel loss and spectral-density coordinate transformations.
- Typo/casing/paraphrase robustness for in-scope prompts.
- Diagnostic underdetermination and explicit mechanism-calibration behavior.

All source-derived training answers are original paraphrases; full paper text is
not ingested. The corpus remains labelled
`PROVISIONAL_RESEARCH_CLAIM_CHECKED_NOT_EXPERT_RELEASE_APPROVED`.

## v2 training profile

`scripts/train_firm4b_v2.py` deliberately starts again from the untouched,
exact pinned Qwen3.5-4B checkpoint.

- BF16 LoRA
- language MLP targets only
- rank 16, alpha 32
- learning rate 1.5e-5
- effective batch size 4
- 1,000 optimizer steps
- 1,024-token sequence limit
- eval every 250 steps
- save every 100 steps
- explicit provisional-data opt-in
- exact train/valid hashes and prompt-leakage gates

The lower LR and broader canonical-fact replay are intended to reduce v1's
destructive factual regressions while rank 16 increases specialist capacity.

## v2 go/no-go criteria

Do not call v2 improved based on training loss. It must beat v1 and the untouched
4B base on held-out evaluation.

Priority release gates:
1. Correct MCT/CZT/InGaAs/InSb identities and detector-material taxonomy.
2. Correct spectral NEP and D* definitions, dimensions and calculations.
3. Correct HgCdTe cutoff-to-bandgap-to-composition reasoning, with temperature/model caveats.
4. Correct CdZnTe vs alternative HgCdTe substrate reasoning.
5. In-scope acceptance despite ordinary typos/spacing errors.
6. High out-of-scope refusal without false refusal of adjacent semiconductor/IR science.
7. No fabricated identities, sources, organizations or measurements.
8. Better private quantitative benchmark performance, especially unseen-family transfer.
9. Diagnostic answers distinguish evidence, hypotheses and insufficient data.
10. CPU/Ollama memory and latency remain practical for a compact 4B model.

No Google GPU should be provisioned until the v2 dataset/trainer commit is clean
and a bounded run package passes local preflight.
